# Balanced diagnostic

Status: specified; not implemented.
Source: [Sibol CSE PRD](../../Sibol-CSE-PRD.md), FR-03.

## Scope and acceptance

Depends on attempt engine and reviewed topic coverage. Offer completion or skip during the learner journey. Default to a configurable 30-question balanced, level-specific diagnostic without repeated items. Reveal explanations after completion.

Acceptance:
- Missing reviewed coverage fails clearly; do not invent results or silently shrink the set.
- Fewer than 10 first-exposure answers for a topic keeps that topic's result provisional.
- Show observed strengths/gaps and sample sizes without official-equivalence claims.
- Skipping produces a balanced starter plan and insufficient-evidence labels.
- Results inform planning while remaining separate from lesson completion.
