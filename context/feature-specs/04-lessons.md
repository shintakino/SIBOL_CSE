# Lesson library and learning progress

Status: implemented and locally verified on 12 November 2026. See [progress tracker](../progress-tracker.md) for evidence and remaining content/release gates.
Source: [Sibol CSE PRD](../../Sibol-CSE-PRD.md), FR-04.

## Scope and acceptance

Depends on published content and owner-scoped accounts. Routes: /learn and /learn/[slug]. Provide concept, worked example, common mistake, technique, and linked practice; support accessible math, tables, passages, diagrams.

Acceptance:
- Every published lesson has topic tags and a version.
- Resume restores the learner's location; completion is owner-scoped.
- Completion and assessed proficiency remain separate records.
- Edits create new published versions and preserve previous versions.
- Unpublished content is unavailable to learners; mobile/keyboard reading works.
